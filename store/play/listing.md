## Full description (max 4000 characters)
Sarawak Trip Planner turns "what should we do in Kuching?" into a day-by-day plan you can book in a few taps.

**Tell us about your trip**
Pick your dates, budget, group and what you love — nature, food, culture, adventure. Our AI planner builds an itinerary for you in seconds.

**Only real, verified places**
The planner chooses from our own catalogue of attractions and local operators. It never invents a place or a price, and every experience is run by a vendor we've checked.

**Book and pay in the app**
Reserve experiences straight from your plan, see the full price up front (in MYR), and pay securely. Seats are held for you while you check out. Your confirmation is right there in your bookings.

**Make it yours**
Not quite right? Add more food, slow the pace, swap things around — your plan updates and stays saved to your account.

**Made for Sarawak**
Kuching waterfront and street food, longhouse and Bidayuh culture days, rainforest and river adventures, national parks — planned by people who know them, bookable directly with local operators.

**Your data, your choice**
Try it as a guest with no sign-up. You can delete your account and data from the Profile tab at any time.
